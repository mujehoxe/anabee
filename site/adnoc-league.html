Introduction

Why protecting the environment is a fundamental human responsibility

Humanity was created with intellect and entrusted with the Earth and its resources — a reciprocal relationship that imposes a duty to protect the environment. Recognizing our impact on nature makes every individual a partner in safeguarding their surroundings.

Excessive consumption has depleted resources, polluted air and water, driven species to extinction, and accelerated climate change — demanding a renewed relationship with our planet. This initiative was born from that awareness: a personal pledge that begins with the individual, extends to community, and reaches the world.

Deep Roots

Environmental stewardship is a value upheld by faiths and civilizations throughout history:
