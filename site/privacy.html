Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ANABEE ("we," "us," or "our") operates anabee.org, an informational website for our smart sustainability ecosystem. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact form submissions. If you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, organization (optional), and message content.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log browser type, device information, IP address, referring URLs, and pages visited.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We may use essential cookies required for site functionality. The 7-second pledge page stores a pledge id in your browser's local storage. If analytics tools are enabled in the future, we will use them to understand aggregate traffic patterns — not to sell your personal data.
  • Voluntary 7-second pledges. If you record and publish at /join, we store your first name, the text you typed, the pillar and place you selected, the video file, the time, and — only if you allow it — a location. The video and first name are shown publicly on anabee.org. We do not verify that the spoken words match the text.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to partnership inquiries, Academy enrolment questions, and general contact requests
  • Operate, maintain, and improve anabee.org
  • Protect against spam, abuse, and security threats
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Legal Basis & Retention

We process contact form data based on your consent when you submit a message, and our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries. We retain contact submissions only as long as needed to handle your request and maintain reasonable business records, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Third-Party Services

Our site may use third-party services such as:

  • Our server — the site and voluntary pledge videos are hosted on a VPS we operate
  • Google Fonts — typography delivery (may receive your IP address)
  • Tailwind CSS CDN — stylesheet delivery

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We select vendors that align with reasonable data protection practices.

BEECOIN & Wallet Features

References to BEECOIN, wallet connection, or blockchain features on this site are informational. When wallet integration launches, separate notices may apply to on-chain interactions. Connecting a wallet does not occur on this marketing site today.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where applicable. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@anabee.org.

Children's Privacy

anabee.org is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Visitors

ANABEE originates from the United Arab Emirates. If you access our site from outside the UAE, your information may be processed in jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
