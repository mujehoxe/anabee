Design System: ANABEE Global Sustainability Initiative (Light Edition)Project ID: 28062026-ANABEE-LIGHT1. Visual Theme & AtmosphereThe ANABEE platform embodies a pristine, gallery-like sanctuary that seamlessly marries organic luxury with state-of-the-art Web3 and AI infrastructure. The interface feels airy, luminous, and expansively spacious, prioritizing generous breathing room, structured clarity, and an elite editorial layout above all else.  The overall mood is profoundly optimistic and transparent. It intentionally moves away from traditional, densely packed dark-mode crypto aesthetics to present a daylight-flooded ecosystem where nature and technology coexist in perfect balance. In alignment with a highly sophisticated visual language, all branding assets and corporate marks (including the ANABEE and 3EC logos) are displayed with absolute simplicity—completely free of backgrounds, borders, or slogans to guarantee an uncompromised, premium presentation.  Key Characteristics:Expansive white space creating a high-end, relaxed editorial rhythm.Soft, whisper-soft diffused shadows and delicate hairline borders to establish clean depth.Photography-first content blocks where crisp, natural lighting acts as a core design element.High-contrast, premium typography optimized for bilingual (English/Arabic) legibility.  Pristine logo integration sitting cleanly on light, minimalist surfaces with zero ambient noise.  2. Color Palette & RolesPrimary FoundationPristine Alabaster Cream (#FCFAF7) – Primary background canvas. An ultra-luxurious, warm white that feels more inviting, soft, and premium than pure sterile white.Crisp Morning Mist (#F3F5F4) – Secondary surface color used for data blocks, interactive card backgrounds, and structural section layering.Accent & InteractiveDeep Velvet Emerald (#0F4C3A) – The primary anchor and CTA background color. Provides striking, highly readable contrast on light foundations while anchoring the ecological theme.Vibrant Mint-Green (#10B981) – Secondary accent used for precise interaction highlights, micro-indicators, positive validation states, and system successes.  Champagne Gold / BEECOIN Accent (#C5A059) – A muted, sophisticated metallic hue reserved exclusively for highlighting tokenomics, digital wallets, and premium asset classes.  Typography & Text HierarchyCharcoal Near-Black (#1A1A1A) – Primary text color for headlines and core body copy, guaranteeing pristine contrast and WCAG AAA reading compliance.Muted Earth-Gray (#5C6160) – Secondary text used for descriptive paragraphs, sub-headers, and contextual metadata to guide the eye without shouting.Soft Border Silver (#E2E5E4) – Tertiary color for elegant borders, structural dividers, and delicate component outlines.3. Typography RulesPrimary Font Family (English): ManropePrimary Font Family (Arabic): TajawalNote: These typefaces ensure perfect bilingual typographic harmony, holding equal visual weight across English and Arabic layouts.  Hierarchy & WeightsDisplay Headlines (H1): Semi-bold weight (600), elegant tight letter-spacing (-0.01em), 3.5–4.5rem size. Used sparingly for high-impact hero sections.  Section Headers (H2): Semi-bold weight (600), 2.25–2.75rem size. Outlines major ecosystem frameworks such as the 7 pillars or technical integration layers.  Subsection Headers (H3): Medium weight (500), 1.5–1.75rem size. Product names, core technologies, and card titles.Body Text: Regular weight (400), relaxed line-height (1.75) for effortless readability, 1.125rem size. Handles comprehensive conceptual copy with ease.Small Text/Meta: Medium weight (500), slightly compressed line-height (1.4), 0.875rem size. Governs financial charts, statistical ratios, and legal references.4. Component StylingsButtonsShape: Modern, subtly rounded corners (8px / 0.5rem radius) to maintain a tailored, geometric look.Primary CTA: Deep Velvet Emerald (#0F4C3A) background with pure white text, featuring crisp, generous padding (1rem vertical, 2.5rem horizontal).Hover State: Smooth 250ms transition to an enriched forest tone accompanied by a soft, diffused lift effect.Secondary CTA: Outlined style with a 1px Deep Velvet Emerald (#0F4C3A) border, transparent background, and a whisper-soft mint tint on hover.Cards & Feature ContainersCorner Style: Gently rounded corners (14px / 0.875rem radius) for refined, modern containment.Background: Solid Crisp Morning Mist (#F3F5F4) or a delicate, semi-translucent white overlay over high-resolution imagery.Shadow Strategy: Flat by default. On hover, a whisper-soft diffused shadow appears (0 4px 20px rgba(15, 76, 58, 0.04)) to simulate subtle physical layered depth.Border: Uniform 1px hairline border in Soft Border Silver (#E2E5E4) to maintain clean geometric alignment.Internal Padding: Generous 2.5rem creating comfortable breathing room for text and data points.Imagery & Corporate IdentityLogo Execution: The ANABEE and 3EC corporate marks must float cleanly on the Alabaster backdrop with no housing box, artificial backdrops, or attached slogans.  Art Direction: Photography must feature natural daylighting, clean editorial lines, macro nature textures, and premium architectural integrations reflecting the UAE's modern landscape. Avoid cliché stock imagery of hands holding soil; instead, opt for high-end rendering and ultra-clear macro shots.  5.
